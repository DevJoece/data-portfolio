# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses a coffee shop's transaction data to uncover customer purchasing patterns, evaluate business performance and generate actionable recommendations that support data-driven decision-making.

Unlike a traditional exploratory data analysis (EDA), this project follows a business-oriented analytical workflow. Each analysis begins with a business problem, followed by hypothesis generation, data validation, exploratory analysis, interpretation of findings and business recommendations.

---

# Business Problem

The coffee shop collects transaction data through its Point of Sale (POS) system but lacks clear insights into:

- Which products drive revenue.
- Customer purchasing behaviour across different periods.
- Monthly sales performance.
- Peak business hours.
- Opportunities to improve revenue through operational and product decisions.

The objective of this project was to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that management can use to improve performance.

---

# Business Objectives

This analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

- Which coffee products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products are purchased most frequently?
- Which months perform best and worst?
- How does customer spending change over time?
- Which periods of the day generate the highest customer activity?
- What factors drive changes in revenue?
- What recommendations can improve business performance?

# Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights:

- 📈 **March generated the highest monthly revenue**, supported by the highest transaction volume.
- ☕ **Americano with milk was the most frequently purchased coffee product** throughout the analysis period.
- 💰 **Latte generated higher revenue than Americano with milk**, showing that the most popular product is not necessarily the highest-value product.
- 📊 Revenue fluctuations were driven primarily by **changes in transaction volume rather than customer spending per transaction**.
- 🕒 Customer purchasing behaviour varied across different periods of the day, highlighting opportunities for better staff scheduling and targeted promotions.
- 🔍 A data quality assessment identified weekday formatting inconsistencies, which were investigated and documented before proceeding with the analysis.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations were proposed:

- Promote higher-value coffee products alongside high-volume products to increase average transaction value.
- Align staffing levels with peak customer periods to improve operational efficiency.
- Use historical transaction trends to improve inventory planning.
- Monitor key business KPIs such as:
  - Total Revenue
  - Transaction Volume
  - Average Transaction Value
  - Revenue by Product
  - Monthly Revenue Trends
- Expand future data collection by recording:
  - Customer IDs
  - Promotion and discount information
  - Product costs
  - Loyalty programme participation
  - Inventory records
